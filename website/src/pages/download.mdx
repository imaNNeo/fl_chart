## Download
I suggest you to download the FL Chart Example App to see how FL Chart works in a real app. This way, you can see the charts in action and understand how to use them in your own projects.
Also, the native app is a great way to test the performance of FL Chart on your device.
At the moment, FL Chart Example App is available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.flchart.app) and [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fl-chart/id6476523019).
Other stores will be added in the future.
(Don't forget to rate the app!)

You can also open the app in your browser here: [FL Chart Example App](https://app.flchart.dev/).
